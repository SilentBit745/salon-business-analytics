
# Business Decision Memo

## Project
Salon Experiment & Causal Analysis

## Objective
To check whether the new business initiative improves customer retention and business performance.

## Key Findings

- Overall customer retention improved by 5.05 percentage points in the A/B test.
- Revenue per visit increased by ₹59.13.
- No-show rate decreased slightly, but the difference was not significant.
- No sample ratio mismatch was detected.
- All analysed customer segments showed improved retention.
- DiD analysis showed positive retention and revenue effects and a lower no-show rate.

## Recommendation: GO WITH MONITORING

The simulated A/B test supports continuing the initiative because retention and revenue improved significantly.

However, the business should monitor revenue, no-shows and customer retention before making a real-world decision.

## Limitations

- All data is simulated.
- The A/B test and DiD datasets are separate.
- Only 22 outlets were included in the DiD analysis.
- Parallel trends cannot be proven by a statistical test.
- Results may differ in a real salon business.

## Final Conclusion

The analysis suggests that the initiative can improve salon business performance. More real-world testing is recommended before full implementation.
